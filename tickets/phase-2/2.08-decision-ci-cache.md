> **Owner:** **D** · **Type:** Decision · **Branch:** `phase-2/decision-ci-cache` · **Blocked by:** 2.07

**Question:** how does the CI retrieval-eval job get a warm `.cache/` (corpus clone + `embeddings.sqlite`) without
blowing the embedding free tier?

**Facts to verify and put in the memo (with links and the date):**
- The free tier for `gemini-embedding-001` counts **texts**: 1K RPD, 100 RPM, 30K TPM (Tech §5.6). A full ingest is
  ~1,045 chunk texts plus ~25 golden questions, which is **over one day's quota**.
- GitHub `actions/cache` rules: caches written on a PR branch are visible only to that branch, while caches written on
  the **default branch** are visible to all PRs. Caches unused for 7 days are evicted. Check the size limits and
  whether a cache is saved when a job fails (`actions/cache/save` with `if: always()`).
- Tech §17 cache key: `hash(FASTAPI_REF, chunking config, EMBEDDING_MODEL, EMBEDDING_DIM)` with a restore-key fallback.
- AGENTS.md §11: `.cache/` is never committed.

**Options to lay out (at least):**
1. A `workflow_dispatch` "warm cache" job on `main` that embeds misses and saves the cache even when stopped by the
   daily quota. Run it on two consecutive days, after which PRs restore it.
2. Upload the locally built `embeddings.sqlite` once as a workflow artifact or release asset that CI downloads (it is
   derived data: 1,045 × 768 × 4 B ≈ 3 MB). Weigh the trust and provenance concerns.
3. Let every run embed its misses with the secret key: simple, but a cold cache fails on the quota.
4. What happens after a 7-day eviction: a documented re-seed runbook.

**Also decide:** the job's behavior on a cache miss that hits the daily quota (fail as an infra error with a clear
message, never as a quality fail), and which secret or variable names CI uses (`GEMINI_API_KEY` secret). `EMBEDDING_MODEL` and `FASTAPI_REF` are **not**
CI variables: they have defaults in `Settings` (R.01).
