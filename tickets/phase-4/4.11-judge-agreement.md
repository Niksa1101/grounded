# 4.11 — Judge–human agreement

> **Before you start:** read [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md), the rules in [tickets/README.md](../README.md) (how to work a ticket, ticket types, [standard checks](../README.md#standard-checks)) and the phase notes in [README.md](README.md). Work only on this ticket.

> **Owner:** **A+G** · **Type:** Build · **Branch:** `phase-4/judge-agreement` · **Blocked by:** 4.10

**What to build:** a measured, published agreement between the judge and the Author on at least 10 verdicts.

**Read first:** Tech §15.4, PRD FR-22, PRD §8 (≥ 0.8 desired).

**Scope notes**
- G: `grounded eval export-verdicts` writes ≥ 10 judge verdicts (mixing SUPPORTED/NOT_SUPPORTED and correctness grades;
  a seeded selection, documented) to `eval/judge_agreement/v1.csv`, with an empty human column. It also computes
  agreement % and Cohen's κ once the column is filled (a pure function, unit tested).
- A: labels by hand **before** looking at the judge's verdicts where possible, then runs the computation.
- The result goes in README Evaluation, with `n`. If agreement is low, say so. Changing the rubric afterwards is a new
  judge prompt version plus a re-run.

**Acceptance criteria**
- [x] The CSV with the Author's labels is committed. Agreement and κ are recorded in the README with `n`. (4.11b, [#85](https://github.com/Niksa1101/grounded/pull/85): `eval/judge_agreement/v1.csv` with its 20 labels, the key `v1.key.json`, the command's output `v1.agreement.md` and the README block; n = 20.)

**Verify:** standard checks.

**Eval impact:** none (measurement of the judge).
