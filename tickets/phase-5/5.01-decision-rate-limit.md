# 5.01 — Decision: rate limiter on serverless, limit and budget numbers

> **Before you start:** read [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md), the rules in [tickets/README.md](../README.md) (how to work a ticket, ticket types, [standard checks](../README.md#standard-checks)) and the phase notes in [README.md](README.md). Work only on this ticket.

> **Owner:** **D** · **Type:** Decision · **Branch:** `phase-5/decision-rate-limit` · **Blocked by:** 4.12

**Question:** on Vercel Functions every instance has its own memory (PRD §12). Does the per-IP rate limiter stay
in memory (D31, per instance) or move to a Postgres-backed counter? And which numbers are used for
`RATE_LIMIT_PER_MIN`, `RATE_LIMIT_PER_DAY` and `DAILY_LLM_BUDGET`?

**Facts to verify:** how Vercel Fluid compute reuses instances (concurrency per instance, how many instances a burst
creates) from current Vercel docs; the Gemini free-tier RPD/RPM for the pinned model (3.01); the Groq limits (4.01).
The budget must sit **below** the free-tier RPD, with a margin for evals that share the key (do they? say so).

**Options:** (a) in-memory per instance, accepted and documented as best-effort; (b) a Postgres sliding-window or
fixed-window counter keyed by `ip_hash` (a new migration, and a DB write per request); (c) a hybrid: memory for
per-minute limits, Postgres for per-day limits.

**Deliverables:** the D31 row amended or confirmed in PRD §11. Tech §12 and DB updated (a migration is *planned*, not
written here). The numbers go in `.env.example` comments or the README, and the values in Vercel env (set by the Author).
Also decide whether `DAILY_LLM_BUDGET` becomes **required** when `APP_ENV=prod`.
